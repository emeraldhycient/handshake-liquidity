Overview

Payout orchestration is the backbone of modern platform finance—a settlement engine that turns a single API call into thousands of local payouts. Handshake ingests bulk disbursement instructions, validates and screens each entry, applies FX, and routes funds through local rails, all while logging every transaction for reconciliation. The result is near-instant settlement at throughput your finance team can rely on, in every market you operate.

From vendor disbursements and creator payouts to affiliate clearings and user incentives, Handshake delivers local-currency settlement with consistency and speed. Our layered architecture breaks each batch into individual instructions—routing every payout to the right corridor, endpoint, and rail—making it ideal for high-volume workflows that once required manual bank files, brittle spreadsheets, and days of waiting on cross-border transfers.

At Handshake, we specialize in designing and operating the payout rails and settlement infrastructure crafted specifically for your platform and business goals. Whether you're paying out to a global marketplace's sellers, a ride-hailing platform's drivers, or a creator-payouts company's contributors, our engine is optimized for throughput, latency, and reliability. We handle the full lifecycle—from API ingestion and compliance screening to FX, routing, and reconciliation into your existing systems.

By combining local rails across 40+ payout corridors with 135+ supported currencies, we help your organization settle faster, cut operational overhead, and expand into new markets with confidence. Our payout infrastructure is built to scale—continuously reconciling every transaction to keep your ledger accurate as your volume grows across borders.
