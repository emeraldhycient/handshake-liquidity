Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Handshake Liquidity Solutions Ltd (“Handshake”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects personal data when you visit our website or use our Services. We act as a data controller for the personal data of our business contacts and website visitors, and typically as a data processor for the payout data our customers submit through the platform.

1. Data we collect

  • Contact data — name, work email, company, and role you provide when you contact us or request a demo.
  • Account & verification data — business details and KYC/KYB information required to onboard.
  • Transaction data — payout instructions, beneficiary details, and settlement records processed on behalf of our customers.
  • Usage & device data — log data, IP address, and cookies collected automatically when you use the site or dashboard.

2. How we use data

We use personal data to provide and secure the Services, route and reconcile payouts, meet legal and regulatory obligations (including anti-money-laundering and sanctions screening), communicate with you, and improve our products. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Legal bases

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on: performance of a contract; compliance with a legal obligation; our legitimate interests in operating and securing the Services; and consent, where required (for example, certain cookies and marketing).

4. Sharing & international transfers

We share personal data with banking and mobile-money partners needed to complete payouts, with service providers who help us run the platform, and with authorities where legally required. Because we operate across borders, data may be transferred internationally under appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

5. Retention & security

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law (financial records are typically retained for several years). We protect data with encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and monitoring, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

7. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies as described in our Cookie Policy.

8. Changes & contact

We may update this policy from time to time and will post changes on this page with a new “Last updated” date. For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@usehandshake.io or use our contact page.
