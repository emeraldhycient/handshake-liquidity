Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Handshake Liquidity Solutions Ltd (“Handshake”) uses cookies and similar technologies on our website and dashboard, and how you can control them.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help sites work, remember your preferences, and understand how the site is used.

2. How we use cookies

  • Strictly necessary — required for the site and dashboard to function, including security and session management.
  • Preferences — remember choices such as language or region.
  • Analytics — help us understand aggregate usage so we can improve the product.

We use only strictly necessary cookies by default; analytics and preference cookies are set only where permitted.

3. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect how parts of the site work. For more on how we handle personal data, see our Privacy Policy.

4. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email privacy@usehandshake.io or reach us via the contact page.
