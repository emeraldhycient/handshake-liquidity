Understanding the settlement layer

A global platform generates thousands of payment instructions every minute, each destined for a different bank or mobile-money wallet. Each instruction is received, screened, and routed to the right local corridor. Similarly, the Handshake settlement engine is built from layers of connected services that work together to validate, orchestrate, and disburse funds. This structure lets platforms settle complex, cross-border payouts in real time, just as a ledger reconciles every movement.

How payout routing works

Routing a payout involves ingesting bulk API instructions and selecting the fastest, most reliable rail for each destination corridor. This process, backed by idempotency keys, ensures every disbursement settles exactly once even when requests are retried. The broader the corridor coverage and endpoint network, the more reliably the engine can reach any bank or wallet, in any supported currency.

Rails and architecture

A modern settlement stack typically includes an ingestion layer, one or more orchestration and compliance-screening layers, and a disbursement layer. High-throughput routing refers to rails engineered to sustain volume without added latency—from validating instructions and applying FX to clearing funds into local accounts. Choosing the right corridor and payout method is crucial for the region at hand.
