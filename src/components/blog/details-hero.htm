How instant payout rails cut settlement to under 2 seconds

Instant payout rails, a cornerstone of modern platform infrastructure, are designed to move money the moment an instruction arrives. Just as a settlement engine ingests bulk API instructions from remote platforms, Handshake Liquidity routes each disbursement through local corridors that connect directly to banks and mobile-money wallets. These rails excel at high throughput, near-instant settlement, and clean reconciliation where legacy transfers struggle.

By routing payouts across 40+ corridors with automatic FX handling, the platform keeps settlement fast and predictable, refining throughput as volume scales.
