For platforms that move money at scale, payout infrastructure is no longer a back-office afterthought—it’s core to margin and trust. Client Overview

Our client, a global marketplace, disbursed to sellers across dozens of markets but relied on a patchwork of local bank integrations. Manual batching, slow settlement, and high per-transfer fees drove up cost and support load—and every new market meant another integration. They needed a single API to route payouts everywhere without rebuilding for each corridor.

Challenges

  • High per-transfer fees across fragmented local bank integrations
  • Slow settlement times leading to seller complaints and support tickets
  • Manual payout batching with limited reconciliation and reporting
  • No unified view of transactions across corridors and currencies
  • Each new market required a separate, costly integration

Strategy & Approach

We ran a phased rollout onto a single settlement engine. We started by consolidating payout routing, then expanded corridor and currency coverage, and finally automated reconciliation and reporting. Each phase was measured against payout cost, settlement time, and success rate.

1 Payout Orchestration

  • Routed bulk disbursements through one API to local banks and mobile-money wallets
  • Idempotent processing and automatic retries for failed transfers
  • Real-time webhooks for payout status and settlement confirmation

2 Corridors & Currency

  • Expanded coverage to 40+ payout corridors with local-currency settlement
  • Automatic FX handling at competitive rates
  • Built-in compliance screening on every payout instruction

3 Reconciliation & Ledger

  • Real-time transaction logging with a unified ledger across corridors
  • Automated reconciliation and exportable reporting
  • Consolidated view of payouts by market, currency, and status

4 Reliability & Scale

  • 99.99% platform uptime with high-throughput settlement
  • Under 2s payout routing for near-instant settlement
  • Scaled into new markets without new integrations
